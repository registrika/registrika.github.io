    Common questions

    Do I need to visit an office at any point?

    No. Registrations and filings under the Companies Act, GST and Income Tax are done online. You upload documents, sign with Aadhaar OTP or a DSC, and receive certificates by email.

    Which is better for me: Private Limited, LLP or OPC?

    It depends on whether you plan to raise investment, how many founders there are, and your expected compliance budget. A Private Limited suits funded or fast-growing businesses, an LLP suits professional and service firms, and an OPC suits a single founder. We'll recommend one after a short call.

    Why are government fees not included in your price?

    They vary by state, capital and entity type, and they're paid to the government, not to us. Showing them separately means you can see exactly what we charge.

    Is this a government website?

    No. Registrika is a private service provider that assists businesses with registrations and filings on official government portals. It is not affiliated with any government department.

    How is my data kept safe?

    Documents are shared over encrypted links, accessed only by the team handling your order, and deleted on request once the work is done, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
